My game

Meta Quest · Multiplayer

Sphere Spoofers

A social VR game for Quest where every player is a little sphere with a face. Hang out with friends in the lobby, pick your colour and expression on the customisation board, then jump into gamemodes together. Earn your way to new stuff in the shop, or just chill and talk. I built it and ran it myself, from the game itself to the updates.
